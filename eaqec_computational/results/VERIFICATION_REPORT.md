## Scope and honesty

Large prime examples are generated from closed formulas; their minimum
distances are not exhaustively enumerated. Independent exhaustive checks
are limited to the stated small fields and the declared enumeration cap.
The program therefore provides computational evidence and exact small-case
verification, not a replacement for a general mathematical proof or a
full literature novelty audit.
